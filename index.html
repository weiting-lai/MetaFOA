05 / System Architecture

Lightweight Spatial Audio Compression, Built to Pair with Any Monaural Codec

Delivering 360° spatial audio at ultra-low bitrates does not require training a monolithic multi-channel neural network from scratch. By combining the physics-grounded Directional Audio Coding (DirAC) framework with a unified 3-D Directivity Vector representation, MetaFOA captures a complete three-dimensional sound field using just 750 bit/s of spatial metadata.

Plug-and-Play with Any Monaural Codec

MetaFOA cleanly separates the omnidirectional audio signal (Wt) from the spatial cues. The audio core can be compressed by any off-the-shelf monaural codec—such as EnCodec, DAC, or future neural audio tokenizers—entirely pretrained and frozen, with zero fine-tuning required.

Unified 3-D Directivity Vector & Fast Fitting

Instead of encoding sound direction (DOA) and room diffuseness separately, MetaFOA merges them into a single 3-D directivity vector and compresses all frequency bands jointly via Cross-Band Residual Vector Quantization (RVQ). The entire codebook fits offline via stage-wise k-means in under 7 minutes without backpropagation.

06 / Spatial Fidelity

How MetaFOA Preserves Fine-Grained Spatial Detail Without Increasing Bitrate

In real-world acoustic scenes, low-frequency ambience and high-frequency sound events arrive from different directions simultaneously. Preserving this spatial clarity requires high frequency resolution—something conventional low-bitrate parameter coders struggle to sustain.

01

Natural Diffuseness Weighting

Each band’s 3-D directivity vector vt,b = √(1 − Dt,b) Ωt,b points in the direction of arrival while its length reflects directional energy. Diffuse background noise naturally shrinks toward the origin, so quantizer capacity is automatically focused on clear directional sources.

02

Why Conventional DirAC Degrades at 750 bit/s

Conventional DirAC [1] divides its bit budget across bands independently. At 750 bit/s, increasing from B = 3 to B = 5 bands starves each band down to just 3 bits (8 coarse directions), causing spatial distortion to rise instead of fall.

03

36-Band Resolution at Zero Extra Bitrate

Because Cross-Band RVQ quantizes the full B × 3 directivity matrix as a unified pattern per frame, the spatial bitrate is completely decoupled from B. Scaling up to all 36 ERB auditory bands costs zero additional bits while faithfully preserving the ground-truth spatial image.

07 / Quantitative Benchmark

Objective Comparison Against Baseline DirAC Across Diverse Acoustic Environments

We evaluate FOA waveform and spatial reconstruction across three benchmarks—SpatialVCTK (anechoic spatial speech), STARSS23 (real-world multi-source sound events), and MEIR (measured reverberant rooms)—pairing both Baseline DirAC [1] and MetaFOA with identical pretrained EnCodec and DAC monaural backbones (6 kbps). At the low spatial metadata budget of 0.75 kbps (750 bit/s), MetaFOA eliminates the severe angular breakdown seen in conventional per-band quantization and even outperforms Baseline DirAC operating at double the metadata bitrate (1.50 kbps) on reverberant recordings.
